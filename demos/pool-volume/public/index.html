What is being compared?

One Ethereum pool and three complete UTC hours. Volume is the sum of absolute token amounts across swaps, in raw integer units. It is not USD volume. Testril binds the factory and reads this pool; its preparation may include factory and dependency history. Allium aggregates this pool's trades through its payment API.

Read/query time includes payment negotiation, polling, and result retrieval. Wallet approval time is shown separately. Testril preparation is separate and may reuse data paid for by someone else. A matching result does not independently verify the underlying events.

Recording playback makes no provider requests. Recordings contain measurements supplied by their operator; they are not independently audited. Missing payment confirmations are labelled, and unknown costs are never shown as verified spending.
